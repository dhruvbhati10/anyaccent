## 5. What the App is, and is not

AnyAccent gives feedback on pronunciation. It is a learning tool.

**Every score is produced by a machine-learning model, and it makes mistakes.** It mishears
sounds, it marks good pronunciation as poor and poor pronunciation as good, and it is less
reliable on a noisy recording, a distant microphone, a very short clip, or an accent unlike
those it was trained on. No person checks a result before you see it.

So please understand what a score is and is not. It is a practice aid. It is **not** a
language test, a qualification, a certification, a measure of your fluency or intelligence,
or evidence of anything about you. Do not rely on it for a decision that matters — a job, a
visa, a course placement — and do not present it to anyone else as if it were an assessment.
We give no warranty that any score is accurate.

When it gets something wrong, please tell us: there is a report button on the practice
screen, and those reports are the main way the model improves.
